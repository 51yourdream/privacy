# Math Practice Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 10, 2026**
**Last Updated: October 10, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for using Math Practice ("the App", "we", "us"). Math Practice is a mental arithmetic practice app for primary school children (grades 1–6). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

We designed the App to work without accounts, advertising or analytics. Your child's learning data stays on the device.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If your child uses the App, please read this policy together with them.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does **not** collect or ask for:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account information (there is no sign-up or login)
- Photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or cross-app tracking data
- Analytics or behavioral data sent to us or to analytics providers

The App does not show ads and does not contain social features, chat or links to share content with other people.

## 3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To make practice work, the App saves the following on your device. This data is **not uploaded** to our servers (we do not operate any server for this App):

- The selected grade
- The number of free questions used today (the daily free limit resets at local midnight)
- Practice session history: date, grade, operation, number of questions, number of correct answers and time spent
- Mistakes: questions answered incorrectly and the answer given (up to 100 items), used by the Mistakes review feature
- A cached copy of your subscription status, so Premium keeps working offline

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the App (see Section 9).

## 4. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Before the purchase screen, the App shows a simple parental gate (a math question for adults), so children cannot start a purchase by accident.

Purchases are handled by the following providers:

- **Apple (App Store)** processes the payment through your Apple ID. We never receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **RevenueCat** verifies purchases and tells the App whether Premium is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier generated on the device, your purchase receipts and subscription status, and basic technical information such as app version, operating system version and App Store country. This information is not linked to your name or contact details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

You can manage or cancel your subscription in your device's Settings under your Apple ID → Subscriptions. The App's terms of use are Apple's standard license agreement: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information on the device is used only to:

- Generate questions that match the selected grade
- Count free daily questions and show practice results
- Show and review past mistakes
- Unlock Premium features when a subscription is active

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by children with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and stores learning data only on the device, no personal information about your child is sent to us.

We follow the principles of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the GDPR / UK GDPR provisions on children's data. If you believe personal information about a child has reached us, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Data Security

Learning data is stored in the App's private storage on the device, protected by iOS app sandboxing and the device's own data protection. Communication with Apple and RevenueCat uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

## 8. Data Retention

- Learning data stays on the device until you delete it or delete the App. The Mistakes list keeps at most 100 items; older items are removed automatically.
- Subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or obtain a copy of your personal data, and to object to processing.

- **Delete learning data**: delete the App. All data stored on the device is removed.
- **Purchase data**: you can ask us to request deletion of the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat by contacting us.

Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription. Please cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

## 10. International Users

The App is available in multiple countries. The only data that leaves your device is the purchase information processed by Apple and RevenueCat, which may be processed in the United States and other countries with appropriate safeguards.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The "Last Updated" date above will change, and significant changes will be described in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, contact us:

We will respond within 30 days.
