# Bead Studio Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

## Photos and projects

Bead Studio processes selected photos on your device. Photos you select or capture, editable patterns, and project settings remain in the app’s local storage. Your system backup settings may include these files. Delete a project to remove its local pattern and source photo from Bead Studio. When you export a file, you choose where to save or share it, and the recipient’s privacy practices apply.

## Purchases

When purchasing is enabled, Apple processes payment and RevenueCat handles Apple purchase history, transaction information, and an app-generated anonymous identifier to verify and restore access and provide subscription analytics. Bead Studio has no account sign-in and does not send photos or pattern files to these services. The app does not use purchase data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Contact

For questions or requests about information handled by Bead Studio, visit the [developer’s support page](https://github.com/51yourdream).
