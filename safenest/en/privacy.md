# SafeNest Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 6, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to SafeNest ("Service", "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use SafeNest, a neighborhood safety awareness app for iOS.

By using SafeNest, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Location Information
- With your permission, SafeNest uses your device location to center map scans, resolve nearby registry records, and calculate distances on your device.
- Location is requested only while the App is in use. We do not continuously track you in the background for advertising.
- You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.
- When you run a scan, the App sends a coordinate and a search radius to our data service so it can return public registry records for that area. We do not build a long-term location history profile about you.

### 2.2 Information You Create in the App
The following stay on your device unless you delete them or uninstall the App:
- Saved places and labels you choose
- Area reports you generate
- Family safety checklist progress
- Daily scan usage counters used for subscription limits

These items are stored locally and are not uploaded to our servers as a personal profile.

### 2.3 Device Identifier
- Each data request may include the vendor identifier that iOS assigns to SafeNest (`identifierForVendor`).
- It is used only for abuse prevention and rate limiting on our data service.
- It cannot identify you by name, and it resets if you delete the App and reinstall later.

### 2.4 Usage and Diagnostics
- We may receive basic technical information needed to operate the Service (for example crash or performance signals provided by Apple platforms).
- Subscription status is checked so the App can unlock Standard or Premium features you purchased.

### 2.5 Notifications (Optional)
- If you enable place alerts, SafeNest may use local notifications to remind you to re-check a saved place.
- Notification permission is optional and can be turned off in iOS Settings.

## 3. Use of Information

We use the information above to:
- Provide area scans, safety scores, map exploration, and address reports
- Persist your saved places, reports, and preferences on your device
- Enforce daily scan limits for subscribed users
- Process and restore subscription entitlements
- Improve reliability, prevent abuse, and provide support

## 4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only when:
- You give explicit consent
- Required to comply with law, regulation, or a valid legal process
- Necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the Service
- Needed by service providers that help us operate the App, under appropriate safeguards

### 4.1 Service Providers
- **Apple**: processes payments and subscriptions through your Apple ID
- **RevenueCat**: receives an anonymous App User identifier and subscription status so SafeNest can unlock paid features. We do not receive your full payment card details
- **Map / geocoding providers** (Apple MapKit): used when you search or reverse-geocode an address
- **Our data API**: receives coordinates and radius to return public registry records for the requested area

Please review those providers’ own privacy policies for their practices.

## 5. Public Registry Data

SafeNest displays information that United States state, territory, or tribal agencies publish for public awareness. That registry content is public by law. SafeNest does not create secret dossiers, and it is not a consumer reporting agency under the Fair Credit Reporting Act. You must not use the App to make decisions about employment, housing, insurance, credit, or similar eligibility.

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the Service, including:
- Encrypted transport (HTTPS) for network requests
- Minimizing what leaves your device
- Limiting server-side use of identifiers to rate limiting and abuse prevention

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please use the App responsibly.

## 7. Data Retention

- Local App data remains on your device until you clear it in Settings or uninstall SafeNest
- Scan requests sent to our data service are processed to return results and are not used to build an advertising profile
- Subscription records are retained by Apple / RevenueCat according to their policies and legal requirements

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal information. Because SafeNest stores most user content on-device, many actions are under your control:
- Revoke location or notification permissions in iOS Settings
- Clear local data from within the App (where available) or by uninstalling
- Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings

For privacy requests, contact us using the email below.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

SafeNest is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your own when you use cloud services that support the App. We take steps designed to ensure such processing complies with applicable law.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will change when we do. For material changes, we may provide additional notice in the App where appropriate.
